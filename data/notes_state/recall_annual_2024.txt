Prison Licence Recalls: Annuual tables to 2024
Publication details
These tables are published as part of the 'Offender Management Statistics Quarterly' publication by the Ministry of Justice.
Link to 'Offender Management Statistics Quarterly' (opens in a new window)
Definitions and measurements
Further details of the terminology used to report prison population statistics can be found in the definitions section for population in the 'Guide to Offender Management Statistics' published alongside these tables.
Geographic coverage
All tables are for England and Wales.
Data sources
The figures in these tables are drawn from the Public Protection Unit Database (PPUD).
Data quality
The figures in these tables have been drawn from administrative IT systems which, as with any large scale recording system, are subject to possible errors with data entry and processing.
Symbols used
[x] Not available
[c] Confidential (disclosure control)
[rev] Revision
[z] Not applicable
Use of notes
Some tables refer to notes. When notes are mentioned, the note marker is presented in square brackets. The note text can be found in the 'Notes' table.
Disclosure control
Where necessary, [c] has been used to suppress values of one or two to prevent the disclosure of individual information. Further disclosure control may be completed where this alone is not sufficient.

Note Description
[note 1] HDC recalls refer to recalls during home detention curfew period
[note 2] In June 2021, Community Rehabilitation Companies (CRCs) were combined with the National Probation Service (NPS) into one Probation Service (PS). We use "National probation" to refer to both NPS and PS.
[note 3] Regional grouping under the new Probation Service replaced the old regional grouping in June 2021.
[note 4] Recall reasons do not sum to the total number of recalls as more than one reason can be recorded against each recall.
[note 5] Some reasons previously included in the 'other' category started to be published separately from July-September 2018. As a result, the 'other' category from this time onwards is not comparable with previous figures.
[note 6] An individual may be counted multiple times if recalled repeatedly in a given year.
